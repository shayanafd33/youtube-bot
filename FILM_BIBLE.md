## 2. Logline

When an experimental memory-erasing therapy escapes a Derbyshire research clinic and turns people into hollow, hunting strangers, a guilt-ridden ex-army medic and an overworked A&E doctor cross the Peak District to reach the clinic's archives, only to find the man behind the outbreak believes he is curing the world, and that their lost friend is still alive somewhere inside the first monster.

Survival horror thriller / human drama (PG-13, no gore)

## 7. Infected / zombie types

**Hollows (standard infected)**: an infected person in everyday torn clothes, pale grey skin, glassy unfocused eyes, slack expressionless face, standing or walking slowly, no gore. Slow, silent, drawn to sound, warmth and humming. Low intelligence. Weakness: cold slows them, they lose interest in silence.

**Fevered (fast infected)**: a feverish infected person, flushed sweating face, wide dilated pupils, hunched sprinting posture, torn clothes, wild expression, no gore. Fast, hunts by sight and sound for hours. Weakness: burns out and collapses after prolonged exertion; sedatives and cold.

**Brutes (mutated infected)**: a hulking infected man with swollen neck and shoulders, flushed steaming skin, vacant eyes, torn rugby shirt, no gore. Rare overreaction in strong, high-adrenaline people. Enormous strength, no pain. Weakness: overheats within minutes; freezing water stops it.

**The Sentinel (Alpha)**: Toby Wren. Keeps fragments of memory, his four-note hum draws Hollows, and he answers to Marcus's voice and the unit call-sign. Weakness: his own remaining humanity.

## 9. World-building bible

- **Location:** Peak District, Derbyshire, and Sheffield, England (fictional Aldersgate Hall near Edale Moor, fictional Haven camp at the disused Ashop water treatment works)

- **Year Season:** November 2027, late autumn, cold, wet, foggy

- **Cause:** Lethe-7, an experimental gene-therapy vector built by Aldersgate Clinical Research to erase traumatic memory in veterans with PTSD. A trial participant relapsed into an infectious form and wandered off the site.

- **Spread:** Bodily fluids (bites, blood, saliva) and, in close confined spaces, aerosol. Spreads across the Hope Valley hospital in hours and across South Yorkshire in days.

- **Government Response:** Day 1 Emergency Alert: stay indoors. Day 2 Operation Hearth: South Yorkshire cordoned by the army. If containment fails, the plan is a controlled firebomb burn of the valley.

- **Military:** Major Helen Hargreaves commands the Edale cordon with orders she increasingly doubts.

- **Medical:** NHS hospitals overwhelmed within a day. Haven, run by Aldersgate under a Ministry contract, offers shelter and 'screening'.

- **Communication:** Mobile networks fail by Day 3. BBC Radio Sheffield broadcasts until its studio is overrun on Day 2. Ham radio and army loudspeakers remain.

- **Infrastructure:** Grid unstable from Day 4. Gravity-fed reservoirs still supply water. Roads blocked with abandoned cars. Fuel scarce.

- **Safe Zones:** Haven (a false sanctuary and a trial site).

- **Danger Zones:** Hope Valley Hospital, Begum Motors garage, Edale valley floor, the Quiet Wing at Haven, Sheffield beyond the cordon.


## 10. Infection rules (never broken)

- **Transmission:** Bites, blood or saliva into wounds or mucous membranes; aerosol only in sealed rooms.

- **Incubation:** Between 20 minutes and 6 hours depending on dose.

- **Stages:** Stage 1 Dissociation: fever, confusion, memory loss (curable by the serum). Stage 2 Fevered: 6 to 12 hours, rage, speed, high temperature (still curable). Stage 3 Hollow: after about 48 hours, temperature falls, the person goes slow, pale, silent and drawn to sound and warmth (not curable).

- **Memory:** Infected lose their identity and memory. Only the Alpha, Toby, keeps fragments.

- **Communication:** No speech. Hollows hum when gathered; Toby's hum draws them.

- **Immunity:** About 1 in 4,000 people carry natural antibodies. Jamie is one.

- **Detection:** Fever above 38.5C and pupils that barely react to a penlight, followed by memory loss.

- **Slowing:** Cold slows the infected and overheats Brutes. Sedatives slow Stage 1 and 2 only.

- **After Death:** Infected do not rise again after death. Death ends the infection. The infected are living people.


## 11. Complete story

**ACT 1: THE BEGINNING**

- Ep 1 *The Man on the Moor*: Mountain rescue volunteer Marcus finds a barefoot man in a hospital gown and army greatcoat on the plateau, hypothermic. The man hums a faint four-note tune and cannot say his own name. Marcus sees the dog tags: WREN T. Toby, his army friend, missing eight months and believed dead. Toby does not know him. Marcus wraps him in his own jacket and starts the long carry down.

- Ep 2 *Aldersgate*: Priya, mid double shift, triages Toby: high fever, pupils that barely react, no memory. Gaz keeps order at the door; Peggy waits with a broken wrist and gets her plaster cast. Two men in grey suits from Aldersgate arrive claiming Toby is a voluntary patient. Priya goes pale at the name. Marcus refuses to release him without proper discharge.

- Ep 3 *Fever*: Toby's fever passes 41 degrees. He stops responding to his name, hums louder, breaks his restraints and attacks a nurse; the camera cuts away before anything is shown. Alarms ring. Marcus and Gaz drag Toby off, staff panic, and the nurse collapses shaking on the floor.

- Ep 4 *Lockdown*: Lockdown fails. The infected nurse and others turn into fast, shrieking Fevered. Marcus grabs his rescue kit and ice axe from his car; Gaz locks a fire door behind them; Peggy cannot run so Marcus carries her to an ambulance. Priya drives. As they leave, the Aldersgate van races the opposite way.

- Ep 5 *Begum Motors*: The ambulance is low on fuel; they stop at a roadside garage. Fatima, wrench raised, nearly attacks them. Her infected apprentice bursts from the workshop; Jamie, hiding in the loft, is scratched on the left forearm as Marcus pulls her away. Fatima admits her brother Imran vanished at dawn.

- Ep 6 *The Long Night*: Priya cleans Jamie's scratch and tells her to stay where everyone can see her tonight. Fatima repairs the ambulance. Over tinned soup Marcus tells Priya about Toby and the army; Priya says nothing about Aldersgate. Gaz argues with Fatima about leaving. Peggy settles them.

- Ep 7 *Radio*: A battery radio carries BBC reporter Rhea Calloway and the government's Operation Hearth: stay indoors, Sheffield cordoned. Marcus's sister Naomi is in Sheffield. Priya wants to find where this started; Marcus wants Naomi; Fatima wants Imran. Jamie's fever never comes. Outside, a faint humming drifts across the yard.

- Ep 8 *The Hum*: Dozens of slow, glassy-eyed Hollows gather silently around the garage, drawn by the hum. The group escapes through the inspection pit and out the back lane. On the radio, Rhea's live broadcast ends when her studio door breaks; she goes silent. Her last words point them to a reception centre called Haven at the old Ashop water works.

**ACT 2: THE SURVIVAL**

- Ep 9 *Turn Back*: The ambulance dies on a moor road. On foot they reach an abandoned army checkpoint with a sign: OPERATION HEARTH - TURN BACK. A loudspeaker voice, Major Hargreaves, orders them away from Sheffield. Marcus argues about his sister; soldiers raise rifles. Hargreaves finally lowers her loudspeaker.

- Ep 10 *Burn Clean*: Hargreaves privately tells Marcus the cordon will 'burn clean' within a week if containment fails. She gives him the route to Haven and warns it is run by Aldersgate under a government contract. Priya, hearing 'Aldersgate' again, looks away.

- Ep 11 *What We Carry*: A quiet evening: Peggy recalls her late husband worked on the Ashop tunnels. Gaz admits he froze once during a knife attack as a police officer and never forgave himself. Fatima confesses she left her brother Imran. Jamie jokes to hide her fear. Priya nearly confesses her own secret but Peggy interrupts.

- Ep 12 *Frequencies*: Following a crackling signal, the group finds Ollie hiding in a radio shop attic with a ham radio. He stammers in panic and nearly gives them away to Fevered outside. Jamie calms him by talking about frequencies. He has been listening to a scientist's coded transmissions.

- Ep 13 *Lethe-7*: Dr Linnea Frost walks calmly out of the mill carrying an orange sample case. She says the pathogen is called Lethe-7, a name nobody in the group has said aloud. Priya freezes. Linnea says a cure may exist in the archives at Aldersgate Hall if they can reach it.

- Ep 14 *The Brute*: A swollen, steaming Brute, once a rugby player, smashes into the barn. Gaz's ribs crack as he is thrown against a beam. Marcus notices the Brute overheating and drives it into the freezing cattle trough; it collapses, shaking. They learn cold is its weakness.

- Ep 15 *Dog Tags*: Priya and Marcus share a quiet moment beside a stove. He shows her Toby's dog tags and speaks about leaving him wounded under fire. She asks about guilt; she lies when he asks about her past. In the dark, Linnea quietly checks a hidden satellite phone, then puts it away.

- Ep 16 *Peggy's Way*: Hollows block the valley route. Peggy leads them to a disused railway ventilation shaft that links to a water tunnel under the ridge toward Ashop. Gaz argues; Peggy's calm authority wins. They descend.

- Ep 17 *Collapse*: In the pitch-dark tunnel, Fevered hunt by sound. Ollie panics, then guides them with the clicks of a handheld radio. A section collapses; Marcus's ice axe is wedged in the rubble and lost as he drags Jamie clear.

- Ep 18 *Haven*: They emerge above Haven: floodlights, fences, tents, tankers. Dr Edmund Rourke welcomes them personally, warm and courteous. He recognises Priya instantly: 'Dr Nair. How good of you to come back.' Priya's face drains; Marcus stares at her.

**ACT 3: THE ESCALATION**

- Ep 19 *Numbers*: Intake staff give everyone numbered wristbands and take blood 'for safety'. Marcus finds his sister Naomi working in the infirmary; they embrace and he is overwhelmed. Naomi says Dr Rourke has saved many lives. Rourke offers the group beds and a tour.

- Ep 20 *Empty Trucks*: Marcus asks Priya how Rourke knows her; she says only that she did locum work years ago. Gaz notes guards never let residents near the east wing; Fatima saw refrigerated trucks arrive empty and leave full. Ollie reports the radio tent is locked.

- Ep 21 *The Signature*: Priya sneaks into the lab and finds the Lethe-7 safety file: her own signature on the sign-off that omitted a cluster of seizures. Linnea steps from the shadows and tells her to close the file. 'Dr Rourke is not a monster. That is what makes him dangerous.'

- Ep 22 *Antibodies*: Priya runs Jamie's blood on a field analyser and finds antibodies nobody else in camp has. Jamie is immune. Priya swaps the sample label to hide it from Rourke's screening and tells Jamie to say nothing. Linnea, watching from the doorway, says nothing.

- Ep 23 *The Quiet Wing*: Gaz picks a service lock and finds rows of sedated people and restrained Hollows being studied. In a glass room sits Toby, calm and humming. Marcus is called; Toby's head turns toward his voice. Rourke appears: 'Your friend is my first success.'

- Ep 24 *The Mercy*: Rourke explains: his soldier son died by suicide. Lethe erases the memory of suffering; Toby feels no pain. Lethe-8, he says, will make the infected peaceful and spare every survivor their trauma. He offers Marcus the same peace. Marcus refuses. Rourke slides across a folder: Priya's signed safety report.

- Ep 25 *Confession*: Marcus confronts Priya. She confesses: two years ago, under pressure, she signed data she knew was incomplete. He says every death is on that signature. Fatima and Peggy argue over whether to forgive her; the group fractures. Priya's glasses are knocked and cracked in the scuffle.

- Ep 26 *Dr Frost*: Rourke's staff come for Jamie for 'follow-up screening'. Jamie says 'Dr Frost told them.' Linnea admits she reported Jamie, believing Rourke could save more people than the group could. Priya slaps her. Fatima takes Linnea's satellite phone. Ollie sees a countdown on a radio tent screen: Operation Hearth, 36 hours.

- Ep 27 *Breaker*: Gaz decides to cut Haven's power to cover Jamie's escape. Fatima shows him the breaker. The lights die; sirens falter; guards scatter. The Quiet Wing's locks, wired to the same circuit, fail.

- Ep 28 *The Door*: Hollows and Fevered pour into the corridor toward the dormitories. Gaz, ribs cracked, holds the fire door so Marcus can get Jamie and the group out. He remembers the day he froze, and does not freeze now. His torch beam is the last thing Marcus sees. Gaz does not come out.

**ACT 4: THE FINAL CONFRONTATION**

- Ep 29 *Burn at Seven*: Survivors watch Haven's lights flicker below. Marcus sits with Gaz's torch; Fatima and Peggy quietly mourn. Naomi stayed with the children in the infirmary. A helicopter passes; on Ollie's radio Hargreaves warns that Hearth will burn the valley at 19:00 the next day unless the outbreak is declared contained.

- Ep 30 *Everything*: Linnea tells everything: Lethe-8 is complete; Rourke plans to dose Sheffield's reservoir supply from the Ashop works to pacify every infected person and erase the trauma of everyone who drinks it. Jamie's antibodies could make a counter-serum. Priya asks Jamie, who chooses to donate.

- Ep 31 *Blood and Wire*: Priya processes Jamie's blood in Linnea's portable centrifuge. Jamie shows fear and humour; Priya promises to stay with her. Ollie builds a relay from radio parts, determined to broadcast whatever they find. Fatima sharpens her wrench in silence.

- Ep 32 *Imran*: Peggy leads them back through the tunnel. In a service room they find Imran, Fatima's brother, in the early stage of infection, huddled and feverish. Fatima refuses to leave him; instead of fighting, she calms him and locks him safely inside with water.

- Ep 33 *First Dose*: Haven is half-ruined but alive; Naomi has hidden thirty children in the infirmary. She and Marcus reunite. Priya gives the first serum to an early-stage child; the fever breaks within the hour.

- Ep 34 *Control Room*: The group confronts Rourke. He calmly explains his Mercy as he starts the Lethe-8 dosing. Linnea turns against him openly. Ollie, hidden, records everything on his headset and patches into the works' public address and radio network.

- Ep 35 *Don't Leave Me*: Rourke uses Toby's hum to hold the Hollows back and guard the valves. Marcus speaks Toby's old unit call-sign. For one moment Toby knows him: 'Marcus. Don't leave me.' Toby turns on Rourke's guards and heaves the dosing valve shut at the cost of his own life in the cold flooded chamber.

- Ep 36 *Stand Down*: Ollie's broadcast carries Rourke's recorded confession and Priya's own admission to every receiver, including Hargreaves's cordon. The helicopters circle, then pull back; Hearth is cancelled with minutes to spare. In the struggle, Rourke is caught in a burst of Lethe-8 vapour.

**ACT 5: THE ENDING**

- Ep 37 *Morning*: Imran wakes after his serum, weak but himself. Fatima weeps. Hargreaves arrives with medics and tells Priya she will be held to account. Priya says she expects it. Marcus holds Naomi.

- Ep 38 *Mercy*: Rourke sits peacefully on a bench, serene and blank; Lethe-8 has erased his grief. He studies his son's photograph and asks Priya who the young man is. Linnea, in handcuffs, watches. Priya looks at her signature and weeps.

- Ep 39 *The Cairn*: Marcus buries Toby's body at the cairn where he first found him. He places the dog tags on the stones and hums the tune. Priya and Jamie stand beside him. Marcus finally says goodbye.

- Ep 40 *Is Anyone There*: Weeks later the serum is being made at scale. Ollie hears a faint voice on the radio from another city: 'Is anyone there?' The group gathers around the speaker. Marcus smiles for the first time. The camera pulls out across the quiet moor.


## 12. Act structure

- Act 1 (episodes 1-8): Day 1, the outbreak at the hospital and the garage; ends with Rhea's last broadcast.
- Act 2 (9-18): the journey across the moor; the group forms; ends with Rourke recognising Priya at Haven.
- Act 3 (19-28): Haven's secrets, betrayals and Gaz's sacrifice.
- Act 4 (29-36): the return, the serum, the control room, Toby's last act and the broadcast.
- Act 5 (37-40): consequences, mercy, goodbye, hope.

## 19. Visual style guide

Grounded British realism: Peak District moors, stone villages, NHS corridors, rain-soaked tarmac, fog. Real fabrics, mud, breath in cold air. Nothing glossy or fantasy.

## 20. Camera style guide

Handheld but controlled; long lenses (85mm) for intimate faces, wide anamorphic 35mm for landscapes, slow push-ins during dread, static frames during grief. Never shaky for its own sake.


## 21. Lighting guide

Overcast natural light, practical sources (torches, hospital fluorescents, stove glow), low-key shadows at night, cold blue ambient with warm skin highlights.


## 22. Sound and music guide

Sound: Sparse. Wind, rain, breath, distant radio. Infected are heard before seen. Silence is used for grief.

Music: Low strings and sustained drones, a single recurring four-note hum (the tune Toby hums) that returns in the finale as a lullaby.
